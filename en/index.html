The ritual

One jar, three moments.

Millésime is not a journal to reread every evening. It is a ritual: a jar that fills up without being touched, and opens once a year.

Every day

You add

A few words and, if you like, a photo, fifteen seconds of sound, a feeling. Ten seconds is enough.

Every month

You receive

Your jar gives you back a random memory. The rest is waiting for you.

December 31

You open

A ceremony lets you relive your year, moment by moment. You pick your favorites.

For when

For every year, especially yours.

In the evening

One last light before turning off the lamp.

The years that matter

A first apartment, an encounter, a big trip.

On gray days

There is always a sunbeam on the floor.

The sound of your memories

Hear it again.

The cicada under the window. A friend’s laugh. The rain on the skylight.

A photo shows, a sound brings you back. Months later, you close your eyes, and you are there.

December 31

The evening everything comes back.

You keep your finger on the jar, night falls, and your year rises. A laugh, the sea in the distance, the sound of a café terrace.

  • In order, at random, or by chapters: the sea, your seasons, your feelings.
  • On the big screen, with the people who lived it with you.
  • At the end, a note to yourself, a year from now.
Share

Your year, in one image.

Once your jar is open, share the card of your vintage: the Lantern, the Constellation or the Label. The year and your jar, never your memories.

The cellar

Your vintages, year after year.

Every opened year is filed in your cellar, its jar still full of light. Browse it by calendar, day by day, or by your favorites.

Rediscover

This day, two years ago.

Your memories come back on their own. Search for a word or a #tag across all your opened years.

Fill in

The years that matter, found again.

A trip, an encounter, a first: fill in a past year with a few memories, guided step by step. Your cellar fills up from day one, and these years can be relived like the others, your way.

Night or day

A light for every hour.

Millésime lives at night. Day, ivory and gold, is waiting if you prefer it. The ceremony, though, is always lived at night.

Just for you

Your memories stay yours.

  • They live in your private iCloud. No one else reads them, not even us.
  • No account to create, no ads, no data collected.
  • Face ID lock if you want it.
  • No streaks to keep, no guilt: an evening reminder, a gentle morning note if you want them, and that is all.
  • Your moments can be exported whenever you like.
